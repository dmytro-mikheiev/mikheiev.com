AI Buyer Persona Agents for Product Research

Role: AI Solutions Architect

Keywords: Marketing Automation Social Media Marketing Automation Marketing Automation Strategy AI Agent Development

Built an AI-powered product research system that uses ChatGPT and automation workflows to create ideal buyer persona agents for a beauty brand.

What it does: Each agent represents a specific customer profile with defined pains, triggers, demographics, buying behavior, and objections. The brand can chat with these agents, ask questions, and send product page links for honest feedback.

Business value: The system helps marketing teams test product positioning, discover customer concerns, improve messaging, and generate better label or packaging ideas before launching or updating products.
