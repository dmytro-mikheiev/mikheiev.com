Automated Video Production Pipeline With Characters

Role: AI Solutions Architect

Keywords: n8n AI Video Generator Video Production Content Creation Software Automated Workflow

Automated AI-powered video generation system built around consistent character, powered by n8n.

The system uses a Notion-based dashboard for configuration, a Hermes AI agent as an automated video editor, and an n8n automation pipeline that handles the full production flow from video rendering to voice synthesis and final assembly.

Videos are generated using Veo 3, Kling, or Seedance. B-rolls are produced with Remotion. Voice is processed through ElevenLabs.

Designed for personal brands and influencers. The pipeline runs autonomously and can produce videos at scale.
