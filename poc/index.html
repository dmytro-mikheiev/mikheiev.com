A note from me

Why I offer this

Conversations at events in Budapest brought me back to the same question: you have an idea for AI or automation, but how do you find out whether it will work for you?

I wanted to make that first step smaller. One problem, a clear scope, and something working that we can look at together.

I’m offering my own time to build it with you. We can test the idea, learn where it helps, and decide what deserves a next step.

Start small

What we can build

One useful slice of a bigger idea. Here are a few starting points we can discuss.

▥

Data analysis & predictions

I can explore your business data, bring patterns into view with charts, and help you understand what the numbers mean. For a focused proof of concept, we can build a small dashboard or test a prediction model on an agreed dataset.

↔

Workflow automation

I look at how your team works, where time goes, and which steps keep repeating. Then I build one automated workflow to connect your tools and reduce the manual work around a specific task.

⌕

An AI agent for your data

I can connect an agent to your documents or business data so you can ask questions in plain language. With access to an agreed tool, it can also carry out a specific task for you, such as looking up a record or drafting an update.

We choose one focused outcome: a small analysis or prediction experiment, one automated workflow, or an agent with one clear job. The €350 scope is agreed before we start, based on the data and tools available.

From conversation to something you can try

How it works

We agree what to test before I start building.

  1. 01

    Talk

    A free initial consultation to understand your idea, your goal, and what you already have.

  2. 02

    Scope & prepare

    I write down the scope and success criteria. You pay €350 upfront and provide the agreed access and data.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    I develop and test the agreed proof of concept within two business days once payment and inputs are in place.

  4. 04

    Handover

    We review it in a final consultation. You receive the working prototype, notes, and source code where applicable.
